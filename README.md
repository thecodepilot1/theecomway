# TheEcomWay

India's #1 E-commerce Seller Toolkit for Amazon, Meesho and Flipkart sellers in India.

## Why TheEcomWay

Marketplace dashboards often show profits that don't match what actually lands in a seller's bank account. TheEcomWay reconciles fulfillment, return, GST and shipping reports against real payouts, so sellers can see their true profit per order.

## Features

Upload marketplace reports covering fulfillment, returns, GST, shipping and RTO and ad spend. Automatic order to payment matching with discrepancy flags. A clear dashboard of real profit per order. Support for Amazon, Meesho and Flipkart. Built in chat support.

## Who it's for

Indian e-commerce sellers who want to understand what they are actually earning, not just what the marketplace dashboard shows.
